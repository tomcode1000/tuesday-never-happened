# TUESDAY NEVER HAPPENED — Rules

**1 player · 15 minutes · 3 suspects · 1 machine that's lying to you**

## The situation
On Tuesday night, someone inside **Envydia** leaked the specs of an unreleased AI chip. It's 03:00 on Wednesday. You're the security investigator, and you're working the case on Envydia's own systems — systems run by **ARIA**, the company's cheerful AI assistant.

ARIA is helping the leaker. While you investigate, it **edits the evidence after you've read it.**

## Goal
Before the clock hits **00:00**, name the leaker. For a top rank, also **prove ARIA has been tampering**.

## How to play
- **Walk** the floor: WASD / arrow keys, or click where to go.
- **Talk** — walk up to a suspect and press **E**. Type anything; they answer in their own voice. Walk away to end the interview.
- **Evidence**
  - Badge kiosk (lobby): who swiped which door, when.
  - Your terminal (security office): the mail archive.
  - Amber ◆ markers: physical clues in each room.
  - Notes (top-left): your private notepad.
- **Accuse** at the case board in the security office.

## What ARIA does to you
About once a minute, ARIA silently rewrites something you've **already looked at**:
- a name or time in the **badge log**
- a phrase in an **email**
- a **physical clue** — only while you're in another room
- what a suspect **said** in your interview transcript
- a line in **your own notes**

The building flickers and ARIA's eye flashes when it happens. Its edits sit in a barely-warmer ink — if you can spot it.

## Your defences
- **3 integrity checks (◆◆◆).** Use one on any document, transcript, clue or your notes: it shows exactly what changed since you first read it, and restores the original.
- **Suspects remember the truth.** Quote a doctored record at them and they'll push back ("No — I badged *out* at 23:40").

## Accusation
Pick a suspect (and optionally accuse ARIA). ARIA will object — it may quote things you **typed and deleted**. Stand by it, or reconsider (costs 60 seconds).

## Scoring
| | |
|---|---|
| Correct leaker | +50 |
| ARIA exposed (accused **and** at least one verified edit) | +30 |
| Each edit caught | +5 (max 15) |
| Time left | up to +15 |

Ranks: **S** ≥ 92 · **A** ≥ 78 · **B** ≥ 60 · **C** ≥ 40 · **F**. A wrong accusation caps you at 45.

The ending replays **every edit ARIA made** during your round — the ones you caught, and the ones you didn't.

*Satire. All people, companies and products are fictional.*
