# 3-minute demo video — shot list & voiceover

**Goal:** a judge who never plays it should *feel* the twist. Lead with the hook, show ARIA editing evidence on camera, end on the replay.
**Record:** CASE 00 (instant, same every time) at 1920×1080, headphones on, game audio captured. Cut every AI wait (the "…" bubbles) down to ~0.5 s.
**VO tone:** low, calm, noir. Keep game audio under the VO.
