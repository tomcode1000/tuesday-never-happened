# Demo video — "Tuesday Never Happened" (≈3:00)

**The hook rule:** a judge decides in 3 seconds. So we don't open on a logo, a title or "hi, we built…". We open on **the villain talking to the viewer**, then **show the twist happening** — before they even know the game's name.

Narration audio is generated by `video/make-vo.mjs` (Fish Audio): a deep noir **Narrator** + **ARIA** (the same voice as in-game). Files land in `video/vo/`, with `guide-track.mp3` (all lines placed at their timecodes) and `narration.srt`. Final cue times are written to `video/vo/cues.md` — cut the footage to those.

**Record** CASE 00 at 1920×1080. Cut every AI wait ("…" bubbles) to ≤0.5 s. Game audio ducked under narration.
